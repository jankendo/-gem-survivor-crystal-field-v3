# Human playtest gate

Status: **NOT YET VERIFIED ON REAL DEVICE**. No human playtest is claimed.

Record tester/device/build SHA/seed/difficulty, chosen loadout, route, death reason and notes. For each item mark PASS/FAIL only after actual play:

- Understand movement/touch and automatic attack within 3 minutes.
- See a build direction by 5 minutes.
- Anticipate evolution/build completion by 10 minutes.
- Feel accomplishment at the 15-minute final boss.
- Exploration offers worthwhile choices without coercive punishment.
- Warp is desirable and its danger/reward is clear.
- Weapon choices are difficult in a productive way; one weapon is not always optimal.
- Enemy attacks and telegraphs remain visible in busy late combat.
- Understand cause of death and actual damage breakdown.
- Want another run after the result screen.

Capture representative weak/median/strong builds, both Standard and Ultra, keyboard/mouse and touch. Automated benchmarks cannot certify these judgments.
